## Business Impact

- Tenant administrators can remove and deliberately restore tenant members without deleting or transferring their global login account; tenant roles and organization relations remain tenant-scoped and are not silently restored.
- Role editing no longer grants unchecked sibling menus through a checked directory ancestor, and historical organization-based internal authorization subjects are normalized to the tenant subject expected by current contracts.
- Workflow recipients receive fewer and more accurate default system/WeCom messages. Deployments without an active external channel continue to use the established cancellation semantics rather than generating duplicate retry traffic.
- File preview no longer depends on embedded-engine ownership of the host bootstrap, and preview-token state follows the shared KV contract for multi-instance use.
- The release contains additive APIs plus controlled Flyway migrations. It publishes platform artifacts only and does not upgrade, deploy, or mutate a business application outside its normal startup migrations.
